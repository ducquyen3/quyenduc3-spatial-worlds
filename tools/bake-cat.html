Bake the hero cat

Turns a picture of a cat into the spheres the hero draws. Pick the picture, check the cut-out, then download cat-data.js and put it next to index.html, replacing the old one. Everything happens in this page; nothing is uploaded.

1. The picture

A plain background is removed by its colour: raise the tolerance until only the cat is left. On a busy background, set the tolerance to 0 and click round the cat to draw its outline instead.

2. The spheres

Pick a picture to start.
